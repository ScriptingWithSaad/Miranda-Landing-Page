# Original design and assets

This is an educational recreation of [Miranda — Paper Portfolio by Niccolò Miranda](https://www.niccolomiranda.com/). The typography, illustrations, project imagery and portfolio content were already referenced in this repository. They remain attributed to their original creators; this repository does not grant additional rights to those assets.

The existing Canopee, Domaine and Editorial New font files are retained. Optimized image variants are in `assets/images`; `assets/image-sources.json` records their original URLs.

- GSAP and ScrollTrigger 3.12.5 are served locally. Copyright and license notices remain in the vendor files. See [GSAP licensing](https://gsap.com/standard-license/).
- Lenis 1.3.26 is served locally under the MIT license. The license is in `assets/vendor/lenis-LICENSE.txt`. [Source](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis).

No external CDN is required for this recreation to display its artwork or run its interactions.
